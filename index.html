Skills · Education · Talent

Building a generation of capable, autonomous talents

Talentuous Minds Fellowship is an initiative dedicated to skills development, education, youth support and the recognition of talent. We create an environment conducive to learning, personal and professional growth, innovation and entrepreneurship.

Building a generation of competent, autonomous talents able to contribute to the development of their community.

What we do

  • Computer and digital skills training
  • Entrepreneurship and leadership programs
  • Data management and analysis services
  • Scientific writing and research support
Our areas of action

Six pillars to develop talent

Our activities are structured around complementary areas, from digital skills to scientific research.

💻

Digital & IT training

General computing, office software, collaborative tools, digital tools for studies and work, introduction to programming and data analysis.

🎯

Personal development

Time management, communication, public speaking, self-confidence, teamwork, project management and problem solving.

🚀

Entrepreneurship & leadership

Business creation, opportunity identification, project design, leadership, team management and innovation.

🔬

Research & academia

Research methodology, scientific writing, LaTeX, bibliographic research, statistics and thesis support.

📊

Data & analysis

Data analysis, data cleaning, survey design, interview guides, data entry forms and methodological support.

🤝

Mentoring

Support for students, researchers and professionals in their academic and career paths.

Our teaching approach

A practical, results-oriented pedagogy

Our approach rests on four principles designed to turn knowledge into concrete skills.

Learn

Acquire the fundamental knowledge required.

Practise

Immediately apply knowledge through exercises and projects.

Experiment

Work on real, concrete situations.

Transform

Use the acquired skills to solve real problems in studies, business or the community.

Who is it for?

A program open to different profiles

Students

Young graduates

Researchers

Entrepreneurs

Professionals

To strengthen skills and the ability to use digital tools.

Organisations
