Module

Interaction analysis

Identification and quantification of protein-ligand interactions, pose analysis and result validation. Production of publication-quality figures.

Duration
2 days (14 h)
Level
Beginner
Price
On quotation
Format
In person or online
⚠️ Programme indicatif. Le déroulé ci-dessous décrit le contenu prévu. Les horaires peuvent être ajustés selon le rythme du groupe. Aucune date de session n'est encore fixée.
Learning objectives

By the end of this module, you will be able to

  • Identify and classify protein-ligand interactions
  • Use PLIP to analyse a complex automatically
  • Compare poses using interaction fingerprints
  • Cluster docking poses to identify representative binding modes
  • Produce publication-quality figures

Prerequisites

  • Module 1
  • Basic knowledge of non-covalent interactions

Required equipment
