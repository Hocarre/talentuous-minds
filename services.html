Services

Data & analysis services

Support for organisations, researchers and professionals in designing studies, collecting, cleaning and analysing data.

📈

Data analysis

Support in organising, processing, analysing and interpreting data.

🧹

Data cleaning

Identification and correction of inconsistencies, duplicates, missing values or errors in databases.

📝

Survey design

Design of questionnaires adapted to the objectives of a study or survey.

🎙️

Interview guides

Development of structured guides for qualitative interviews and field studies.

🗂️

Data entry forms

Design of tools facilitating structured data collection and entry.

🧭

Methodological support

Assistance in study design, data collection and preparation of datasets for analysis.

Computational services

We run your simulations for you

You don't have the computing power, the installed software or the time? We take charge of your simulations and deliver the analysed results.

🖥️

Docking on demand

You provide a target and a list of molecules: we prepare the files, run the docking and deliver the scores, poses and analysed interactions.

📦

Library screening

Virtual screening of compound collections (ZINC, PubChem, in-house libraries), with ADMET filtering and prioritisation of hits.

⏱️

Long molecular dynamics

Simulations from 100 ns to several microseconds, stability analysis, interactions over time and binding free energy calculation.

🧩

Structure modelling

Structure prediction with AlphaFold or homology modelling, model validation and preparation for your docking studies.

🧪

ADMET filtering

Assessment of absorption, distribution, metabolism, excretion and toxicity properties. Lipinski's rules, bioavailability prediction and elimination of problematic compounds before laboratory testing.

📄

LaTeX scientific writing

Formatting of your articles, theses and reports with LaTeX: IMRaD structuring, booktabs tables, multi-panel figures, BibTeX bibliography and compliance with journal requirements.

Process

How a service engagement works

Scoping

You describe the biological question, the target and the molecules of interest. We assess feasibility and required computation time.

Quotation

We send you a quotation specifying the scope, the deadline and the expected deliverable.

Execution

File preparation, running the simulations, quality control and regular progress updates.

Delivery

Analysed results, figures, tables and a reproducible methodological report. Raw files are provided.

ℹ️ Ce que nous ne faisons pas. Nous ne réalisons pas de tests expérimentaux en laboratoire (synthèse, essais biologiques). Nos prestations couvrent uniquement la modélisation et l'analyse computationnelle. Les résultats prédits doivent être validés expérimentalement avant toute conclusion thérapeutique.

Molecular modelling

Drug Discovery services

Beyond training, we support research teams in their computer-aided drug discovery projects: from target preparation to results analysis.

🧬

Molecular docking

Receptor and ligand preparation, grid definition, AutoDock Vina docking, validation by redocking and RMSD calculation.

🔎

Virtual screening

Building and preparing compound libraries, automated screening, ADMET filtering and prioritisation of hits.

⏱️

Molecular dynamics

Simulating complex stability, RMSD and RMSF analysis, interactions over time, binding free energy.

🔗

Interaction analysis

PLIP analysis, interaction fingerprints, pose clustering, consensus scoring and publication-quality figures.

🧩

Structure prediction

Obtaining and validating 3D structures with AlphaFold, ESMFold or homology modelling, preparation for docking.

⚙️

Automated pipelines

Developing Python scripts to automate preparation, batch execution, analysis and report generation.
