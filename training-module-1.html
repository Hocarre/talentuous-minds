Module

Molecular docking

Principles of docking, receptor and ligand preparation, search grid definition, AutoDock Vina parameterisation and interpretation of affinity scores.

Duration
2 days (14 h)
Level
Beginner
Price
On quotation
Format
In person or online
⚠️ Programme indicatif. Le déroulé ci-dessous décrit le contenu prévu. Les horaires peuvent être ajustés selon le rythme du groupe. Aucune date de session n'est encore fixée.
Learning objectives

By the end of this module, you will be able to

  • Explain the principles of docking and the limitations of scoring functions
  • Prepare a receptor and a ligand in PDBQT format
  • Select an active site and define a relevant search grid
  • Parameterise AutoDock Vina according to the biological question
  • Validate a protocol by redocking and interpret the RMSD
