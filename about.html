About Talentuous Minds

An initiative oriented towards skills development, education, youth support and the recognition of talent.

Who we are

An initiative for talent development

Talentuous Minds Fellowship, presented as Talentuous Minds, is an initiative oriented towards skills development, education, youth support and the recognition of talent.

Through its activities, Talentuous Minds seeks to create an environment conducive to learning, personal and professional growth, innovation and entrepreneurship.

The organisation focuses particularly on developing the practical skills that young people, students, researchers, entrepreneurs and professionals need to better meet the demands of the academic and professional world.

Its documented activities include computer training, entrepreneurship and leadership programs, as well as training and services related to data management and analysis. Public records also attest to scientific training activities, notably around scientific writing and digital tools.

Our vision

Our vision

Building a generation of competent, autonomous talents able to contribute to the development of their community.

The vision of Talentuous Minds rests on the idea that the sustainable development of a society depends above all on the development of its human capital.

The organisation therefore aims to contribute to the emergence of a generation that is:

  • better trained;
  • more autonomous;
  • able to use technology;
  • able to undertake;
  • able to solve problems;
  • open to innovation;
  • committed to its community;
  • able to turn knowledge into concrete solutions.

Talentuous Minds thus seeks to help bring forth talented minds capable of thinking, innovating and acting.

Our mission

Our mission

Develop skills and recognise talent through training, support, mentoring, entrepreneurship, digital tools, research and knowledge management.

Concretely, Talentuous Minds seeks to:

  • offer accessible and practical training;
  • strengthen young people's digital skills;
  • support students and professionals;
  • promote entrepreneurship;
  • develop leadership skills;
  • encourage research and scientific output;
  • support organisations in data management and analysis;
  • promote the use of technology in education and work;
  • create learning and professional development opportunities.
Our values

The principles that guide us

Seven values shape our identity and our way of working.

01

Excellence

We encourage our members and beneficiaries to strive for excellence in their academic, professional and entrepreneurial paths.

02

Integrity

We place particular importance on honesty, responsibility and respect for ethical principles.

03

Innovation

We encourage new ideas, the use of technology and the creation of solutions adapted to the problems of our society.

04

Sharing

Knowledge gains more value when it is shared. Talentuous Minds therefore encourages the transmission of knowledge and mutual aid.

05

Leadership

We want to help train people able to take initiative, lead projects and have a positive impact on their environment.

06

Commitment

We encourage our members to actively participate in the development of their community.

07

Inclusion

We want to create learning opportunities accessible to different profiles, notably young people, students, researchers and professionals.
